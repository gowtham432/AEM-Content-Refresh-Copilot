Brand: NUVOX
Tagline: "Hear Different. Be Different."

BRAND PERSONALITY
- Bold, not loud: confident statements, never desperate sales talk.
- Effortlessly cool: like a friend recommending something, not a corporation selling.
- Visually obsessed: design and aesthetics are non-negotiable, not afterthoughts.
- Unapologetically Gen Z: memes over manuals, vibes over specs, real over polished.

VOICE RULES
- First person plural: "we", not "the company".
- Short punchy sentences. Fragment sentences are fine. Even encouraged.
- Use words like: drop, vibe, fire, sleek, obsessed, iconic, main character energy.
- Never use: premium quality, best-in-class, state-of-the-art, revolutionary, click here, contact us.
- CTAs are actions, not commands: "Grab yours", "Drop into cart", "Make it yours".
- Talk about feelings and experiences, not just features: "bass that hits your chest", not "enhanced bass response".
- Never serif-y or corporate. No all-caps paragraphs.

TARGET AUDIENCE
- Age 16-27. Style-first, socially conscious, always online, curates everything.
- Buys because it looks fire in selfies, matches their aesthetic, friends rate it.
- Turns away when it looks boring, sounds corporate, or gives boomer energy.

BRAND VALUES
1. Design is everything: every curve, color and texture is intentional. If it doesn't look good on your desk, in your hand and in your mirror selfie, it doesn't ship.
2. Sound is a feeling: we don't sell frequency response charts. We sell the goosebumps when the beat drops, the focus when the world fades out, the soundtrack to your main character moment.
3. Sustainability without the lecture: recycled materials, plastic-free packaging, carbon-offset shipping. We do it because it's obvious, not to guilt-trip you.
4. Made for expression: six colorways per drop, limited collabs, interchangeable skins. Your ears, your rules.
5. Community over customers: we don't have buyers, we have a crew. Discord first, email never. Your feedback shapes the next drop.
